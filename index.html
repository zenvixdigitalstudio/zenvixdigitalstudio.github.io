OUR STORY

About Zenvix Digital Studio

Zenvix Digital Studio is an independent digital assistance initiative based in Berhampur, Odisha. Our aim is to make commonly used online services easier to discover by bringing useful service categories and official portal links together in one convenient workspace.

We focus on clear guidance, organized access and a simple digital experience. Zenvix is privately operated and is not affiliated with any government department or authorized Common Service Centre.

OUR COMMITMENT

Clear, responsible digital assistance

Zenvix is an independent service and not an official government website, CSC centre, PAN centre or Aadhaar centre. Government applications, eligibility, fees and approvals are controlled by the relevant authorities. Aadhaar holders must personally control their credentials, OTPs, biometric authentication and payments. We do not offer enrolment, biometric updates, AEPS or operator-only Aadhaar services.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. This website provides information and links to third-party official portals. When you choose to open an external portal, that portal's own privacy policy and terms apply. Do not send passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar details, payment credentials or other sensitive information through public social media or unsecured messages. Any information voluntarily shared for assistance should be used only for the requested purpose, handled carefully and not retained longer than necessary. Contact us if you have a privacy question or wish to request correction or deletion of information you have shared with us.

Terms & Conditions

By using this website, you understand that Zenvix Digital Studio is an independent digital assistance service and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government department. Links are provided for convenience and lead to external websites that are operated by their respective organizations. Portal availability, eligibility, official fees, processing times and final decisions are determined solely by those organizations and may change without notice. Users should verify all details on the official portal before submitting an application or payment. Assistance does not guarantee approval or a particular outcome. Users remain responsible for the accuracy of information submitted and must personally control their passwords, OTPs, biometric authentication and payments. Use of this website must comply with applicable laws and the rules of each external portal.
